Frontmatter Explained

Metadata at the top of a file.

Frontmatter is a YAML block between two lines of --- at the very top of a file.

---
title: "My First Post"
date: 2026-01-15
tags: [markdown, guide]
draft: false
---

Static site generators read it to set the title, date, layout and more.
