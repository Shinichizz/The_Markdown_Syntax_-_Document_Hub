Escaping Characters

Show literal symbols that Markdown would otherwise interpret.

Put a backslash before a special character to display it literally.

\*not italic\*
\# not a heading
1\. not a list

Characters you can escape - \ backslash, backtick, * asterisk, _` underscore - {} [] () brackets, # + - . ! |
