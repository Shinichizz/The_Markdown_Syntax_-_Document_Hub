Keyboard Shortcuts for Markdown

Write faster (Ctrl on Windows/Linux, Cmd on Mac).

ActionShortcut
BoldCtrl + B
ItalicCtrl + I
Insert linkCtrl + K
Inline codeCtrl + E (Typora: Ctrl + Shift + `)
Preview (VS Code)Ctrl + Shift + V

Shortcuts vary by editor; check your editor's keybinding settings.
